Privacy Policy

Stream Clipper · Effective November 21, 2026

Stream Clipper is a personal tool built and used only by Rawad Fahd to upload short video clips to his own YouTube channel, Reelish. This policy explains what data the app handles.

What the app accesses

When I sign in with my Google account, the app asks for one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload. It uses that permission only to upload videos and set their title, description and privacy status on my own channel. It does not read, change or delete any other data in the Google account, and it does not access other users' accounts.

What is stored

The OAuth token Google issues is stored locally on the machine that runs the tool, so it can upload without signing in each time. It is never sent to any server other than Google's, never shared and never sold. The app does not collect analytics, cookies or personal information from viewers or anyone else.

Public stream content

The tool downloads publicly available Twitch broadcasts and clips to make highlight videos. Each uploaded video credits the original streamer with a link to their channel. It does not collect personal data about streamers or their viewers.

Google API Services

Stream Clipper's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Its use of YouTube API Services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security page under Third-party apps & services. Deleting the local token file also removes the app's stored access.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: reach me through github.com/rfahd1525.
