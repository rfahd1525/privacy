Stream Clipper

A personal tool that turns highlights from Twitch streams into vertical, captioned clips for my own YouTube channel.

What it does

  1. Finds recent streams and popular viewer clips from streamers who allow clipping.
  2. Picks out the high-energy moments and transcribes them.
  3. Cuts each moment into a 9:16 Short with captions and credits the streamer.
  4. Uploads the finished clips to my own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

Who it's for

This tool is used only by its developer, to upload to a single YouTube channel that the developer owns. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.

Google and YouTube data

The app requests one permission, youtube.upload, used only to upload videos to my own channel. See the privacy policy for details. This app's use of YouTube API Services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service.
